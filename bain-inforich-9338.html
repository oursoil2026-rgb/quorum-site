Bain Capital's Take-Private of INFORICH / ChargeSPOT (9338)

INFORICH Inc. (ChargeSPOT) · TYO:9338 · Acquirer: Bain Capital via BCJ-102 + management

A Bain Capital vehicle, BCJ-102, reported 88.60% of INFORICH (9338, ChargeSPOT); together with a company director who rolled over his stake, the group reached 97.82%. The tender offer closed 2026-03-31, at a total cost of about ¥40.7 billion.

What the filing shows

INFORICH is a management-rollover take-private: insiders stay in, Bain provides the capital, and bank debt covers the rest. The "important proposal activity" box reads not applicable even though the stated purpose is to take the company private — because the deal runs through the tender-offer-and-consolidation route, not the "important proposal" mechanism the box asks about.

What the filing does not say: the squeeze-out price for remaining minority holders, or whether it draws an objection.

Part of a pair

INFORICH is one of two Japanese companies Bain Capital moved to take private in the same 2026 filing window — the other being MCJ (6670), through a different vehicle (BCPE Meta Cayman, L.P.).
