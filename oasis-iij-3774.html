Oasis Management's 7.48% Stake in IIJ (3774)

Internet Initiative Japan Inc. · TYO:3774 · Activist: Oasis Management Company Ltd. (EDINET E31883)

Oasis Management Company holds 7.48% of Internet Initiative Japan (IIJ, 3774) — 13,723,994 shares, acquired for about ¥36.4 billion — disclosed in a May 2026 large-shareholding report that states its purpose as "portfolio investment and important proposal activity." What the filing shows

The report gives the holding, the date, the cost, and the stated intent to engage. A meaningful share of the position was built through an off-market block rather than slow open-market buying — a detail that reads as deliberate construction, not drift across the 5% line.

What the filing does not say: the actual proposals, the board's response, or whether IIJ is one theme among Oasis's five simultaneous positions or a standalone bet. Those are the next entries in this timeline.

Part of a five-company sweep

IIJ was one of five Japanese companies Oasis crossed 5% in within a single week (filed May 2026): IIJ (3774, 7.48%), Kanadevia (7004, 5.44%), KYB (7242, 5.63%), Ichikoh (7244, 5.39%) and Nichirei (2871, 5.01%). Each filing carries the same stated purpose.
