Bain Capital's ¥155.7bn Take-Private of MCJ (6670)

MCJ Co., Ltd. · TYO:6670 · Acquirer: Bain Capital via BCPE Meta Cayman, L.P.

A Bain Capital vehicle, BCPE Meta Cayman, L.P., reported 69.56% of MCJ (6670) — 70,792,445 shares — after a tender offer that closed 2026-04-07. Total cost was about ¥155.7 billion: roughly ¥62.3bn of equity and ¥93.4bn of bank debt, with the acquired shares pledged back to the lending banks.

What the filing shows

The filing records the holding, the tender-offer completion, the financing split, and — days later — that all the acquired shares had been pledged to the syndicate. What it does not say is the price minority holders ultimately receive in the consolidation, or whether the squeeze-out draws an objection. Those are the next entries in this timeline.

Part of a pair

MCJ is one of two Japanese companies Bain Capital moved to take private in the same 2026 filing window — the other being INFORICH / ChargeSPOT (9338), through a different vehicle (BCJ-102).
